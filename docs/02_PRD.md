# LifeOps AI — Product Requirements Document

## Problem
People receive information that requires action, but the action is often buried inside emails, notices, PDFs, screenshots, forms, and messages. Summaries reduce reading effort but do not reliably convert information into a completion plan.

## Solution
LifeOps AI converts unstructured real-world information into an evidence-backed action plan with deadlines, prerequisites, dependencies, risks, and next steps.

## Target users
Global everyday users: students, job seekers, employees, freelancers, families, senior citizens, and small-business operators.

## Value proposition
Instead of asking “What does this document say?”, users can ask:
“What exactly do I need to do, by when, and what do I need before I start?”

## MVP
- Text/PDF/image input
- AI extraction
- Structured actions
- Deadlines
- Requirements
- Dependencies
- Risks
- Evidence
- Confidence
- Checklist
- What-if questions
- .ics calendar export
- Action Pack export
- Delete session

## Non-goals
- Automatic legal/medical/financial decisions
- Automatic form submission
- Payments
- Email sending without explicit user confirmation
- Full enterprise document management

## Success criteria
A first-time judge can understand the product within 30 seconds and complete the full workflow within 2 minutes.

## Competitive differentiation
Not “summarize my document”.
The product creates a verifiable execution graph and portable Action Pack.
